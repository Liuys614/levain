A sourdough timer built for a bake that takes all day

Most kitchen timers count down once. A real loaf needs eight stages across eighteen hours, half of them overnight. Levain was built for that.

One timer, every stage

Chain autolyse, mix, bulk fermentation, folds, shape, cold retard and bake into a single proofing schedule. Every step shows the clock time it will actually happen before you start — so you know tonight whether you are pulling bread out at 7am or at noon. Long bakes get Next Day markers where the timeline crosses midnight.

Alarms that actually wake you

Stage alerts ring through silent mode and Focus, the way a Clock alarm does. Loud alarms are set per stage: quiet for the overnight retard, loud for the oven.

Live on your Lock Screen

The countdown lives in the Dynamic Island and on your Lock Screen. Glance at the stage name, the step number and the time left without opening the app.

Dough that runs late

When a bulk fermentation stage ends, the clock keeps counting up instead of resetting. Dough is ready when it is ready — Levain waits, and tells you how far over you are.

Build your own bread, with baker's percentages

Drag stages into a timeline, scrub durations on a rotary dial, and drop ingredients, temperatures and humidity targets onto any step. The baker's percentage calculator runs as you type, with levain counted as half flour and half water.

A sourdough baking log of every loaf

Log a bake and it becomes a photo on your calendar. The background is lifted off your loaf on device, so the bread sits as a sticker on the day you baked it. Notes, a gallery, the ingredients and the timeline it really ran on are kept as a snapshot — editing the recipe later never rewrites history.

Questions

Will the alarms wake me during an overnight proof?

Yes. Stage alerts use AlarmKit, so they ring through silent mode and Focus the way a Clock alarm does. Loud alarms are set per stage, so you can keep the overnight cold retard quiet and make the oven timer loud.

What happens when a stage runs past its timer?

The clock keeps counting up instead of resetting. Dough is ready when it is ready, so Levain waits and tells you how far past the planned time you are.

Does Levain need an account or an internet connection?

No. There is no account and no sign-up, and your recipes, bakes and photos stay on your iPhone. The only time Levain reaches the network is on first launch, to download the two sample recipe photos. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How is baker's percentage calculated?

Percentages update as you edit ingredients, with levain counted as half flour and half water — the standard assumption for a 100% hydration starter.

What does the free version include?

Three recipes and five logged bakes, with the full multi-stage timer, loud alarms and the Live Activity. Levain Pro removes both limits.

Can I cancel the free trial?

Yes. Cancel any time from Settings › App Store › Subscriptions on your iPhone. As long as you cancel at least 24 hours before the 7-day trial ends, you are not charged.
